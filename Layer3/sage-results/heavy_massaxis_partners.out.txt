[A] Spectrum of (i g^mu d_mu + i g^4 d_4 - M) psi = 0 on y >= 0
    Chiral split psi = f_L(y) psi_L(x) + f_R(y) psi_R(x), 4D mass m:
      (d_y + M) f_L = m f_R,  (-d_y + M) f_R = m f_L,  edge f_R(0) = 0
  [PASS] m = 0: f_L = exp(-M y), normalisable; f_R = 0 meets the edge condition
  [PASS] f_R = sin(k y) with m^2 = M^2 + k^2: bounded for every real k > 0
    For m < M the solutions grow or decay as exp(+-sqrt(M^2 - m^2) y);
  [PASS] sinh(q y) solves the f_R equation with m^2 = M^2 - q^2 < M^2
  [PASS] the only m < M solution meeting f_R(0) = 0 is sinh, which diverges: no bound state
    => one chiral zero mode, then a CONTINUUM of vector-like (L+R) states
       from m = M. Not a Kaluza-Klein tower: the half-line is not compact.

[B] Gauge couplings between zero mode and continuum
  [PASS] continuum: m f_L = M sin(k y) - k cos(k y), and (d_y + M) f_L = m f_R closes
  [PASS] int f_L^0 f_L^k dy = 0: a y-independent gauge field cannot turn a zero mode into a partner
    So W, Z, photon and gluons produce partners only in PAIRS (threshold 2M);
    single production needs Yukawa mixing, suppressed by (m_f / M)^2.

[D] Data
    M >~ 1 TeV  =>  zero-mode width 1/M <~ 2.0e-19 m along x^4

  [PASS] the zero mode sits within gamma* < 1e-11 kg/m^3 of the edge
    So x^4 of a field mode cannot be the ambient density of the matter
    the particle sits in (water is at x^4 = 2.7e-5 m, where exp(-M y) ~
    exp(-1e14)). The edge reading (Q4) needs only gamma >= 0, not this.
  [PASS] M y(water) > 1e13 for M = 1 TeV

NET.
  * DT's 5D field on the half-line has one chiral zero mode and, above it, a
    CONTINUUM of vector-like partners from m = M. Gauge bosons produce them
    only in pairs. These are DT's heavy mass-axis states.
  * DT does not fix M. Williams' own normalisation (M = m_f) puts charged
    partners at the fermion mass, excluded by LEP and the Z width (a Williams
    discrepancy under the recovered, Yukawa-mass reading). DT's own density
    scale 1/K_gamma gives M ~ hbar H0/c^2: excluded. Any other density
    gamma* gives M = a0/gamma*, and physical densities span every M, so
    picking one is choosing M.
  * Data bound M >~ 1 TeV (indicative: the LHC bounds are for discrete
    vector-like states; a continuum without a mass peak needs a recast).
  * Consequence for the reading: x^4 of a field mode is not the ambient mass
    density; the zero mode lives within ~1e-11 kg/m^3 of the edge.
  * Verdict: NO PREDICTION. M is a free parameter with a lower bound. The
    qualitative signature, a continuum rather than a tower, would distinguish
    DT from compact extra dimensions if partners were ever seen.
