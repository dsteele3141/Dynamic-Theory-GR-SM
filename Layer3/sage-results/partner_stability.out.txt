partner_stability.sage -- is pair production of partners exact?
==========================================================================

[A] Couplings that are independent of x^4 conserve the partner label
    A 4D field B(x) independent of y couples modes a, b with weight
    int f_a f_b dy, so it can change a zero mode into a partner only if
    the two profiles overlap.
  [PASS] gauge field or 4D metric: int f_L^0 f_L^k dy = 0 (zero mode -> partner forbidden)
    The same weight governs the graviton: with g44 fixed and the 4D metric
    independent of x^4 (the GR recovery), T_ab couples modes by int f_a f_b.

    Yukawa  y_f H(x) psibar_L(block 1) psi_R(block 2): block 1 has the
    left-handed zero mode, block 2 the right-handed one (e.g. L and e_R).
      zero_L(1) x partner_R(2):  -(M1 - M2)*k/(M1^2 + k^2)
      partner_L(1) x zero_R(2):  (M1 - M2)*k/(M2^2 + k^2)
  [PASS] zero-mode/partner Yukawa overlaps are proportional to M1 - M2
  [PASS] with ONE native mass M for all blocks the Yukawa cannot convert a zero mode either
  [PASS] with one M the partner profiles of the two chiralities coincide, so the
        Yukawa is diagonal in k (orthonormal continuum: int = delta(k - k'))
    => With one native M and every 4D field independent of x^4, each
       fermion line keeps its mass-axis label k: partners are created and
       destroyed only in pairs, and the lightest one cannot decay.

[B] What breaks the rule
  [PASS] (i) block-dependent M (M1 != M2): a partner decays through the Yukawa, rate ~ y_f^2 (M1-M2)^2
  [PASS] (ii) a Higgs localised along x^4 (profile exp(-mu y)): overlap -k mu/((M+mu)^2+k^2) != 0
    (iii) the drift (velocity along x^4 ~ H0 x^4) varies slowly against
          the gap M between zero mode and continuum: H0 / M = 1.5e-45 at 1 TeV
  [PASS] the drift is adiabatic: zero-mode/partner mixing < 1e-40, negligible
    DT does not say whether M is one constant or differs between blocks
    (heavy_massaxis_partners: DT does not fix M). Case (i) is a free choice.

[C] The lightest partner, for one native M
    The partner continuum of a 5D field carries that field's gauge
    representation in BOTH chiralities (vector-like): the lepton-doublet
    block gives a doublet (nu', e'), Y = -1/2, the e_R block a singlet, Y = -1;
    quark blocks give coloured partners.
  [PASS] nu' has Q = T3 + Y = 0 but Y != 0: it couples to the Z
    Electroweak loops make the charged doublet member heavier (~0.35 GeV
    for M >> M_Z), so the lightest LEPTONIC partner is nu', a Dirac
    SU(2)-doublet fermion with a vector Z coupling. Its right-handed
    component is a doublet member, NOT a sterile right-handed neutrino.
    Quark partners keep baryon number and k, so the lightest coloured
    partner is stable too.

[D] Data (nu' as the dark matter; imported limits, indicative)
    Z exchange on xenon: sigma_n = mu_n^2 G_F^2 (Q_W/A)^2 / 2pi = 2.3e-39 cm^2
  [PASS] sigma_n ~ 2e-39 cm^2 (the Goodman-Witten value for a Dirac doublet)
    LZ limit ~ 3e-47 cm^2 x (M / 1 TeV)  =>  excluded as all the DM for
    M <~ 8e+10 GeV
  [PASS] Turok's 4.8e8 GeV is excluded for nu' by > 10x margin, even if the limit is 10x weaker
  [PASS] the thermal-relic mass (~1.1 TeV) is excluded by direct detection by ~1e8
    Above M ~ 1.1 TeV a thermal relic overcloses: at M = 8e+10 GeV by 5e+15.
  [PASS] for every M >~ 1 TeV a thermal population of stable partners is excluded
    So stable partners are allowed only if they were never thermally
    produced: reheating temperature well below M.

[E] A right-handed neutrino in DT
    Each 5D block takes one of the two Lorentz-invariant edge conditions
    (noflux_boundary_derivation); e_R is the right-handed zero mode of
    a block with the other condition. A gauge-singlet block with that
    condition gives a right-handed zero mode with T3 = Y = 0:
  [PASS] its charge Q = T3 + Y = 0 and it has no gauge coupling: a sterile nu_R
    So nu_R needs no compactified mass axis, only one more block (the
    same price as in the SM). Its Majorana mass and any symmetry making
    it stable are 4D inputs, as in Turok's proposal; DT adds nothing to them.

NET.
  * The pair rule is EXACT under the conditions of the recovery: one native M
    for every block and all 4D fields (gauge, metric, Higgs) independent of
    x^4. Gauge and Yukawa overlaps between a zero mode and a partner vanish,
    and the drift mixes them at H0/M < 1e-40. So the lightest partner and
    the lightest coloured partner are STABLE.
  * It is broken only by choices DT leaves open: block-dependent M (Yukawa
    decay ~ (M1-M2)^2) or a Higgs localised along x^4.
  * The candidate is nu', the neutral member of the lepton-doublet partner
    continuum: a Dirac doublet with Z couplings, NOT a sterile nu_R. Direct
    detection excludes it as the dark matter below ~1e11 GeV (Turok's
    4.8e8 GeV included); a thermal relic overcloses at every M >~ 1 TeV.
  * Consequence for the manuscript (Sec. 6.2): with one M the partner sector
    does not simply decouple. It needs M above the reheating temperature, or
    block-dependent M so that partners decay. A bound on M, not a candidate.
  * A sterile nu_R is available in DT exactly as in the SM: one gauge-singlet
    block with the right-handed edge condition. No compactification needed.
